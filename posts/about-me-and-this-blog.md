I'm Elias Charbel Salameh, an R&D engineer at ADERA in Bordeaux. I work on
mixed AI / model-based approaches for low-consumption, certifiable embedded
automotive algorithms. It is the same research project as my upcoming CIFRE
PhD with Stellantis and the IMS laboratory, started ahead of time.

## Where I'm coming from

- **Education.** A B.E. in Electrical & Electronics Engineering at the Holy
  Spirit University of Kaslik (USEK), then a double-degree M.Sc. in Complex
  Systems Engineering (Automation & Robotics of Intelligent Systems) at the
  Université de Technologie de Compiègne (UTC).
- **Control under uncertainty.** As an R&D intern at the OpenLab
  Stellantis–IMS–SANPSY, I worked on Adaptive Cruise Control for connected
  automated vehicles: robust MPC, soft-constrained MPC and reinforcement
  learning, benchmarked across speed profiles, and a Safe-RL controller with
  Control Barrier Functions.
- **Robotics.** My final-year project at USEK studied a SLAM pipeline for the
  Unitree AlienGo quadruped, fusing a 3D LiDAR, an IMU and an Intel RealSense
  D435i depth camera.
- **Computer vision.** At inmind.ai academy I built an end-to-end
  object-detection pipeline for BMW assembly-line items with YOLOv5
  (98.4% mAP).
- **Teaching and community.** I lead lab sessions on linear systems dynamics
  at the Université de Bordeaux, and I was Head of Judges for the World Robot
  Olympiad Lebanon 2025, coordinating 42 judges.
